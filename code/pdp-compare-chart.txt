IMPORTANT: Please complete Step: 0 before editing your theme.

Step: 0
Safety first: duplicate your theme (works for any Shopify theme)
- In Shopify admin, go to Online Store > Themes.
- On your current live theme, click ... (or Actions) > Duplicate.
- A copy appears under Theme library. Click the name to rename (e.g., "CRO setup - Draft").
- Work on the duplicate only: click Actions > Edit code.
- Use Actions > Preview on the duplicate to test changes safely.
- Publish the duplicate only after you verify everything works.

Theme-agnostic notes for finding files
- Product section file: commonly sections/main-product.liquid (may be sections/product-main.liquid, sections/product.liquid, or similar).
- Product form snippet: commonly snippets/product-form.liquid (may be snippets/main-product-form.liquid or similar).
- If unsure, in Edit code use the search bar to find "product-form", "add to cart", or "Buy buttons".

Step: 4
Go to product form snippet: snippets/product-form.liquid (or your theme's equivalent)
Paste this code:
  {%- for block in section.blocks -%}
    {%- case block.type -%}
      {%- when 'cro_compare_chart' -%}

Final steps (Assign and test):
1. Assign to a product:
   - Go to Products > open a product.
   - In the Metafields section, find "CRO | PDP | Compare chart" and select the metaobject entry you created.
   - Save the product.
2. Test on the duplicate theme:
   - In Online Store > Themes, click Actions > Preview on your duplicate.
   - Open the product you assigned and verify the compare chart appears.
3. Publish when ready:
   - When everything looks good, click Actions > Publish on the duplicate theme.

Troubleshooting:
- Chart not visible: Ensure the product metafield is set and the block was added in the correct product section/snippet as described in the steps above.
- Empty rows/text: Make sure the Metaobject fields (title, content, headings, lists) are filled.
- Cannot find files: Use the search bar in Edit code to look for "product-form" or "main-product".
- "Mixed reference" not available: Use "Reference" > "One CRO | PDP | Compare chart" instead.
